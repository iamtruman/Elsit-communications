About Elsit

An electrical and communications firm built for Kenya's growing sites.

Elsit Communication has worked at the front line of Kenya's electrical and communications sector, giving clients a single, contemporary source for information security, power systems, networking, telecommunications, instrumentation & control, and IT support. We work with organizations that need those systems to be planned together, not bolted on one at a time.

Our team is based in Nairobi and takes on work across the surrounding region, from first-fix electrical wiring to fully monitored security networks.

Why we exist

Mission & vision

Our mission

To deliver dependable electrical, telecommunications and IT infrastructure that keeps Kenyan businesses powered, connected and secure — installed properly the first time, and maintained for the long run.

Our vision

To be the trusted partner for integrated power, network and security engineering across Kenya and the wider East African region, known for technical quality and consistent follow-through.
