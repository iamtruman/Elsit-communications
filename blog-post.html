A note from the Managing Director

Elsit Communication provides advanced technological solutions that suit the growing needs of our clients in Kenya. That only works if we get the relationship right, not just the installation.

We place real importance on engaging honestly with everyone a project touches — client staff, site teams, regulators and the communities around a site. That means being accountable for our work and holding ourselves to clear standards of professionalism, in line with national and international practice.

Our clients are the best evidence of how we work: cohesive, transparent relationships that move their objectives forward, not just ours. As we grow, we're committed to building projects in a way that's sustainable for the client long after our team has left site.

"We aim for an environment that encourages innovation and delivers for our stakeholders — making the best use of resources, improving efficiency, and finding better ways to work."

Hillary Yegon — Managing Director

That includes respecting the people we work alongside: free, informed consent on how a site is used during works, and a real channel for clients and communities to raise concerns during a project, not just at sign-off.
