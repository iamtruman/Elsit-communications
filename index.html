Who we are

One contractor for power, network and security.

Elsit Communication has worked across Kenya's electrical and communications sector, helping organizations modernize the infrastructure they run on. Rather than handing a client to three different contractors for wiring, networking and surveillance, we plan and deliver all three as one connected system — so the CCTV feed, the office network and the backup power actually work together.
